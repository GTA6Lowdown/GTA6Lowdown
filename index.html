Before you play

GAMEPLAY TIPS

What Rockstar has told us about how GTA 6 plays, and what it means for your GTA 5 habits. More tips once the game is out.

01

Change your outfit to lose the cops

Police track your outfit and your car. Buying and wearing a new outfit removes your Heat, and switching cars or splitting up Jason and Lucia helps too.

Rockstar's Rob Nelson, via TGG

02

You can't carry the whole gun shop

Two concealed handguns and two long guns, with only one long gun on your back. Everything else you've bought stays in your personal vehicle.

Rockstar's Rob Nelson, via TGG

03

The game remembers how you play

Your Criminal Profile tracks consistent behaviour, which Rockstar says may affect how the world reacts to you and how the story plays out. There's no right or wrong way to play.

Rockstar

04

Take the radio with you

Radio and on-demand podcasts play on foot, through headphones on Jason's or Lucia's phone.

Rockstar

05

Launch night checklist

Preload done, up to 212GB free, and it unlocks at midnight your local time. Grab the free digital map of Leonida on launch day.

Rockstar Newswire

The notebook

GTA 6: WHAT WE KNOW

When does GTA 6 come out?

19 November 2026, at midnight local time, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Preload opened on 10 November.

Who are the main characters?

Two leads: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Rockstar says an easy score goes wrong and the pair end up in a criminal conspiracy across Leonida.

Watch: who is Lucia Caminos?

Where is GTA 6 set?

The state of Leonida, including Vice City and the Leonida Keys.

How much space does it need?

Up to 212GB, according to Rockstar's preload information.

Watch: the launch guide

Which radio stations are confirmed?

Six so far: Cocoteo, Back Country, Afro Bank FM, The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM and Dirty South Classics. You can also listen on foot, through headphones on your phone in the game.

Watch: the radio stations

Is there a PC version?

Not announced. An early-2027 PC date is a rumor, not something Rockstar has said.

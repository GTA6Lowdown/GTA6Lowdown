Before you play

GAMEPLAY TIPS

What Rockstar has told us about how GTA 6 plays, and what it means for your GTA 5 habits. More tips once the game is out.

01

Change your outfit to lose the cops

Police track your outfit and your car. Buying and wearing a new outfit removes your Heat, and switching cars or splitting up Jason and Lucia helps too.

Rockstar's Rob Nelson, via TGG

02

You can't carry the whole gun shop

Rockstar's Rob Nelson says you can't hide a whole arsenal on you any more. Long guns go in your vehicle's trunk or an Ammu-Nation locker. Previews mention two handguns and two long guns on you, but Rockstar hasn't given exact numbers.

Rob Nelson (Rockstar North) interviews; previews

03

The game remembers how you play

Your Criminal Profile tracks consistent behaviour, which Rockstar says may affect how the world reacts to you and how the story plays out. There's no right or wrong way to play.

Rockstar

04

Take the radio with you

Radio and on-demand podcasts play on foot, through headphones on Jason's or Lucia's phone.

Rockstar

05

Launch night checklist

Pre-load done (up to 212GB, depending on your console), and it unlocks at midnight your local time. Grab the free printable Leonida map from Rockstar's GTA VI site on launch day. Full launch day guide

Rockstar Newswire
